# Ping Privacy Notice

Last updated: 26 July 2026

## Summary

Ping 1.0 is an offline-first subscription tracker. It does not require an
account and does not connect to a bank.

## Data stored on the device

Ping stores the subscription details entered by the user, display currency,
onboarding state, notification preference, and a small summary used by the app.
This information remains in the app's local storage.

## Notifications

Ping requests notification permission only after the user enables bill
reminders. The app schedules local notifications for upcoming renewal dates.
Disabling reminders cancels all notifications scheduled by Ping.

## Export

When the user chooses CSV export, Ping creates a text representation of the
subscription list and opens the operating system share sheet. Data is sent only
to the destination selected by the user. The selected third-party destination
is governed by its own privacy terms.

## Collection and sharing

This version contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, bank connection,
online account, or Ping-operated backend. Ping does not sell subscription data.

Opening an external cancellation page or the project's source-code page leaves
the app and is governed by the destination website.

## Retention and deletion

Data remains on the device until the user clears it, uninstalls the app, or the
operating system removes the app's storage. “Clear all data” removes locally
stored subscriptions and cancels scheduled reminders.

## Future features

Any future cloud sync, bank connection, analytics, or payment feature requires
an updated privacy notice and explicit user consent before release.

## Contact

Privacy questions and reports can be submitted through the project's GitHub
issue tracker: https://github.com/bambi2008/ping/issues
